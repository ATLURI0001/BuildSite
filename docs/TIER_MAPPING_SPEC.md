# Architectural Progression & Commit Mapping Specification

## Progression Curves
Progression follows a logarithmic curve to reward long-term continuous contribution:

- **Tier 1 (0 - 50 commits)**: Foundation pouring, excavation, site surveyors.
- **Tier 2 (51 - 200 commits)**: Steel structural framing, concrete slabs, rough plumbing.
- **Tier 3 (201 - 600 commits)**: Exterior glazing, solar arrays, roof cladding.
- **Tier 4 (601 - 1500 commits)**: Luxury interior, infinity pool, modern landscape architecture.
- **Tier 5 (1501+ commits)**: Smart estate with dynamic time-of-day lighting and weather events.
