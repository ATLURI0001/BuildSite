# Environment Lighting & Material Presets Reference

## Time-of-Day Lighting Profiles
- **Dawn (06:00 - 09:00)**: Soft warm directional key light (Color temperature: 3200K, Sun angle: 12°).
- **Noon (11:00 - 15:00)**: High-contrast daylight (Color temperature: 5600K, Sun angle: 75°).
- **Golden Hour (17:00 - 19:00)**: Warm long shadows (Color temperature: 2800K, Sun angle: 8°).
- **Midnight (21:00 - 05:00)**: Cool ambient moonlight with warm architectural interior emission.
